## **[First-affiliation country distribution](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/mapviewer/index.html?webmap=77a29015d02b4dd1a56ac203f98461eb).** 

This interactive map shows the first-listed affiliation countries of the 106 primary studies included in our review of deep learning for LiDAR-based forest tree species classification. Each paper contributes one count to its first-listed affiliation country, covering 20 countries in total. Country polygons are shaded by paper count, with darker colours indicating more papers across five classes: 1, 2–3, 4–6, 7–11, and 12–54. Clicking a country displays its paper count, percentage of the review corpus, and associated references.

## **[Study sites and datasets](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/mapviewer/index.html?webmap=8ebcfb0a06e44a3e8851aeb5b2c9161f).** 

This interactive map provides a developing geographical inventory of study sites and datasets reported in the reviewed literature. The current version contains 80 paper–site records from 50 of the 106 primary studies, covering 42 mapped locations. Pop-ups provide available information on tree species or classes, sample units and scope, data types, dataset sources, DOI links, and coordinate provenance. Some locations represent approximate study areas or the midpoints of reported geographical bounds. Coverage and attributes remain incomplete and will be expanded as verification proceeds; records sharing a location or dataset retain their individual paper associations.
